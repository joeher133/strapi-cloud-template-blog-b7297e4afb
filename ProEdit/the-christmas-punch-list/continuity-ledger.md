## Stated Discrepancies
1. Delivery driver present when Brian spoke: driver "was gone to his other stops" before Brian arrived at seven-thirty (ch 1) | Nico says Brian told her "in front of me and the delivery guy" (ch 2).
2. What Nico praised to the front booth on Sunday: Olivia says Nico "told my booth on Sunday it was the best stair work he'd ever seen" (ch 5) | Sunday scene shows Nico praising the door casing to the booth before Olivia arrived (ch 2).
3. Days Nico had baked at Lourdes's by ch 5: Nico first bakes at Lourdes's on Tuesday, Twenty-four days (ch 4) | "baked in Lourdes's hot-corner oven for four days," Twenty-two days (ch 5).
4. Old stair width: 29 1/2 in. clear between plaster and rail (ch 5) | Elijah: "two feet wide" (ch 5).
5. Days remaining in ch 8: Humberto: "Twenty days?" (ch 8) | Tara: "Nineteen." (ch 8).
6. Time until the bolo de frutas is ready: "Two more weeks" (Fátima, ch 8) | "Three weeks" (Nico, ch 8).
7. Who drove Tara to Warren: Nico drove (ch 8) | Tara: "She drove me to Warren" (ch 9).
8. Time the hood went up: at six (ch 10, ch 11) | "Six-forty" (Nico, ch 11).
9. Nico's boot size: bought twelve (ch 14) | Tara says eleven (ch 14).
